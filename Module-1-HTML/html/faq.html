Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform, accessibility standards, and web development topics.

Account & Registration

How do I create a new account on the platform?

You can create an account by visiting our Registration Page, filling out the required personal and contact details, and clicking "Complete Registration".

Is my personal phone number and email secure?

Yes, all submitted personal data is encrypted and handled strictly according to standard data protection regulations and privacy guidelines.

Can I update my profile details after signing up?

Absolutely. You can update your profile information, tech preferences, and contact details anytime through your Account Dashboard settings.

What should I do if I forget my account password?

Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to receive a password reset link via your registered email address.

Web Standards & Accessibility

What is W3C Validation and why is it essential?

W3C Validation ensures that HTML code adheres to official syntax standards set by the World Wide Web Consortium, improving browser compatibility and site accessibility.

How does keyboard navigation work on this site?

You can use the Tab key to move through links, form controls, and interactive elements. Press Enter or Space to activate buttons or toggle details sections.

What are ARIA attributes used for?

Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) attributes help communicate state, roles, and properties to assistive technologies like screen readers when standard HTML is insufficient.

Why are skip links included at the top of pages?

Skip links allow keyboard and screen reader users to bypass repetitive navigation menus and jump straight to the main page content.

E-Commerce & Checkout Process

Which payment methods are accepted on the checkout page?

We accept major credit and debit cards, UPI payments, and net banking options through our secure payment gateway.

How can I track the delivery status of my order?

Once your order is processed, a tracking number and link will be sent to your registered email address for real-time updates.

Can I save my shipping address for future orders?

Yes, checking the "Save shipping details" option during checkout securely stores your address for faster checkouts in the future.

What is your policy on returns and refunds?

Orders can be returned or refunded within 14 days of delivery, provided the items remain unused and in original packaging.
